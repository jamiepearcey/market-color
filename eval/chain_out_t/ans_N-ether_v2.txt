Ether fell about 8% over the past week—dropping 8.2% over seven days to roughly $1,587—driven primarily by a weakening Japanese yen (at a 40-year low) that boosted the US dollar and pressured risk assets like crypto [4][6]. The selloff was compounded by muted onchain demand, shrinking transaction fees, and caution from Strategy's plan to potentially sell over $1 billion in bitcoin [4]. Ether-specific pressure also came from $274 million in Ether ETF outflows that week [8] and "window dressing," as investors reduced holdings in assets that had fallen over the prior three months ahead of quarter-end [2]. More broadly, the crypto market faced steady spot Bitcoin ETF outflows, a hawkish Fed under Kevin Warsh, a dollar at 12-month highs, and capital rotating into AI-linked semiconductor stocks [9]. Note the context gives slightly varying figures for the weekly decline (8.2% [4] vs. 8.8% [5]), but all point to the same drivers.
