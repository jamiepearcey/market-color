Ether fell roughly 8% over the past week (down 8.2% to about $1,587) as broad crypto weakness gripped the market [2][4]. The main driver was renewed U.S. dollar strength—the Japanese yen slid to a 40-year low and USD/JPY broke above 162.00, its highest since 1986—which pressured risk assets like crypto [4][8]. Dollar strength was itself tied to markets repricing Federal Reserve rate expectations, with investors prioritizing portfolio protection over fundamentals [6][7]. Additional pressure came from muted onchain demand, shrinking transaction fees, and Strategy's plan to potentially sell over $1 billion in bitcoin, which added caution to an already thin market [4]. Quarter-end "window dressing," as investors trimmed holdings in assets that had fallen over the prior three months, also contributed [2].
