The process — two modes, honestly marked

A standing index (1–3), then per notable move a decomposition routes to one of two modes: firm-grounded attribution for idiosyncratic moves, or a breadth-ranked sector/macro overview for the non-idiosyncratic ones. Everything below now runs on live data.

Both modes now run on live data: the router splits every move on live prices, the idiosyncratic path serves grounded-or-abstained firm news, and the sector/macro path gives the big picture — what’s driving the market, not just a firm-by-firm answer. Caveat: theme breadth leans on dictionary entity-linking, which has a noise floor; the clusters & volumes are solid, the per-sector counts approximate.
