1 · The centring check — uncentred vs mean-centred

Transformer embedding spaces are anisotropic: uncentred cosine similarity is dominated by proximity to the corpus centroid, which tracks coverage volume, which tracks firm size — which predicts co-movement for reasons unrelated to text. Mean-centring removes it. All measures are on a correlation scale.

Read with care. This is second-moment — co-movement, not returns and not direction. One regime, one outlet, one market. The winning configuration was chosen by looking at outcomes across many variants, so it is exploratory, not confirmatory: nothing here is out-of-sample yet. Effect sizes are small in absolute terms (an IC of 0.05). Sparse idf vectors are not anisotropic, so the centring artifact cannot arise in the lexical space at all — which is part of why it is the more trustworthy representation.
